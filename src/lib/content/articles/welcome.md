I rebuilt this site to do one thing well: hold the writing I care about.

For years my notes lived in scattered docs, half-finished threads, and drafts I
never shipped. This is the place they go now — cleaned up, dated, and easy to
share. If a piece is worth reading twice, it belongs here.

## What you'll find

Mostly essays and field notes at the intersection of **construction technology**
and **marketing**: what's actually working, what's hype, and what the people
building both are learning the hard way.

Some pieces will be long. Some will be a single observation. All of them will be
things I'd want to send to a colleague.

## How it works

Every article is a Markdown file. Publishing is as simple as writing the piece,
adding a title and description, and pushing it live. Each one gets its own share
card, so when someone drops a link in a Slack channel the preview does the
selling.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
